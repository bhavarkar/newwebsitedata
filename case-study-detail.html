The Challenge

Physio One Indore was receiving over 100 inquiries a day on WhatsApp. During treatment hours, the clinicians couldn't reply, and the front desk was constantly juggling phone calls and physical patients. Patients would often wait 4-6 hours for a reply about booking a slot, leading to significant abandonment.

The Technical Solution

We designed a multi-layer automation ecosystem using n8n and Google Gemini. Unlike a simple chatbot, this system has access to the clinic's knowledge base and its live Google Calendar.

📱

Inbound WhatsApp API

Captures messages in Hindi, Hinglish, or English.

🧠

Gemini 1.5 + Pinecone RAG

Processes medical FAQs and identifies booking intent.

📅

Google Calendar Integration

Checks live availability and creates appointments.

🔔

Staff Telegram Alerts

Pings the therapist's phone the moment a new booking is confirmed.

The Results

  • ✓ 300+ appointments booked fully automatically in month 1.
  • ✓ 98% accuracy in answering treatment-related questions.
  • ✓ Front desk stress levels reduced, allowing for better in-clinic patient experience.
