Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our WhatsApp AI Agents.

Will the WhatsApp AI Agent understand broken English or Hinglish?
Yes! The core strength of our WhatsApp AI chatbot development is utilizing LLMs tuned for regional dialects. It fluently parses typos, mixed languages, and complex intents effortlessly.
What is the tech stack used for these AI bots?
We rely on an enterprise-grade stack including the official WhatsApp Cloud API, OpenAI / Google Gemini models, n8n for workflow orchestration, and Supabase for tracking AI memory.
Does the WhatsApp bot hallucinate or give wrong answers?
No. We implement precise Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) constraints. This means the AI support agent only answers based purely on the documents and data you provide us. If it doesn't know, it respectfully transfers to a human.
How quickly can you deploy a WhatsApp AI Automation?
A standard deployment, incorporating custom prompts, CRM linkages, and testing takes between 2 to 4 weeks depending on workflow complexity.
