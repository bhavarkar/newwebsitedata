Pricing Questions

How do you decide the price for my project? ▼
Every project is scoped individually based on your industry, number of integrations, conversation volume, and support requirements. We never charge a flat rate because no two businesses are the same.
Do you charge in INR or AED? ▼
Both. Indian clients are quoted in INR. UAE clients in AED. We also work with international clients in USD.
Is there a monthly cost after the setup? ▼
Yes — we offer optional monthly retainers for ongoing support, monitoring, and optimisation. This is always quoted separately and is never mandatory.
Can I pay in instalments? ▼
Yes. We typically structure projects as 50% on start and 50% on delivery. Flexible arrangements are available for larger projects.
What if I need changes after launch? ▼
All packages include a post-launch support period. Changes within scope are included. Out-of-scope additions are quoted separately and approved before any work begins.
Can I see full deliverables before committing? ▼
Absolutely. Your proposal includes a complete deliverables list, timeline, and scope before you confirm anything.
